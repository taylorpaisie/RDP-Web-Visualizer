References & citation

Ave Satanas.

This site visualizes RDP5 CSV exports; it does not re-run recombination detection. Cite RDP5 itself, and cite method-specific papers when the underlying analysis uses those methods.

RDP5 software

Martin DP, Varsani A, Roumagnac P, Botha G, Maslamoney S, Schwab T, Kelz Z, Kumar V, Murrell B. (2021). RDP5: a computer program for analyzing recombination in, and removing signals of recombination from, nucleotide sequence datasets. Virus Evolution 7(1): veaa087.

DOI RDP home page RDP5 manual
Original RDP method

Martin D, Rybicki E. (2000). RDP: detection of recombination amongst aligned sequences. Bioinformatics 16(6): 562–563.

DOI PubMed
Method-specific citations: RDP5 includes multiple detection methods such as RDP, GENECONV, Bootscan/Recscan, MaxChi, Chimaera, SiScan and 3Seq. The official RDP page lists the paper to cite for each method; use those citations when reporting analyses that rely on them.
